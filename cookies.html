Cookie Policy

A website-specific explanation of the current cookie position, third-party resources and the controls available in your browser.

Effective and last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy reflects the technologies currently used by this website. If analytics, advertising, personalisation or other tracking tools are introduced, this policy and any legally required consent controls will be updated accordingly.

Current cookie position

Alomaritech.xyz does not currently use first-party advertising, behavioural analytics or personalisation tools, and does not set first-party tracking cookies.

This policy explains the technologies used on this website and how third-party resources may interact with your browser.

02

What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small pieces of data that a website or service can ask a browser to store. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, SDKs and server-side identifiers. They can support essential functionality, preferences, measurement, security or advertising depending on how a site is built.

03

What this website currently uses

We do not currently set first-party analytics, advertising or personalisation cookies. It also does not use local storage or session storage for tracking. Navigation, pricing selection, FAQ interaction and page animations operate without persistent browser identifiers.

04

Third-party resources

The website loads certain resources from external providers, including web fonts, an icon library and hosted images. When your browser requests a third-party resource, the provider necessarily receives network-level information such as the request, IP address and browser headers. A third-party provider may also apply its own cookie or similar-technology practices independently of Alomari Tech.

Because those practices are controlled by the relevant provider and can change, review the provider’s own policy if you want detailed information about a particular external service.

05

Your browser controls

You can use browser settings to block, delete or limit cookies and site data. Blocking technologies used by third-party providers may affect whether externally hosted fonts, icons or images load as intended.

06

If analytics or marketing is added later

If Alomari Tech later introduces analytics, advertising, personalisation or other non-essential cookie-based functionality, this policy should be updated before or when that change is deployed. Where applicable law requires notice or consent before non-essential tracking, appropriate controls should be implemented rather than relying only on this text.

07

Contact and updates

Questions about website privacy or cookies can be sent to alomaritech@gmail.com. The effective date will be updated when the website’s cookie or tracking behaviour materially changes.
