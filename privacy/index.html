About this policy

This policy explains how Alomari Tech handles personal information received through alomaritech.xyz and related business enquiries. It applies to visitors who contact us through the website or by email.

This policy concerns the public website. An agreed client engagement may require separate privacy or data-handling arrangements.

02 / 10

Information we collect

When you enquire about our services, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, organisation, project requirements, preferred timing, and any information you choose to include in your message.

Technical information, including an IP address, browser information and request timestamps, may also be processed when you access the website or its associated services.

03 / 10

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange consultations, assess project requirements, prepare proposals, maintain business correspondence, protect the website and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Submitting an enquiry does not, by itself, enrol you in a marketing mailing list.

04 / 10

When information is shared

We may use providers that support website delivery, enquiry processing, email communications, security and business administration. These providers may handle information needed to perform those functions. We may also disclose information to professional advisers or where required by law.

Information submitted through our enquiry forms is not displayed publicly. If you would like further information about providers involved in handling an enquiry, contact us using the details below.

05 / 10

International handling

Some service providers may process or store information outside Australia. Where applicable privacy law imposes obligations concerning overseas disclosures, those obligations will apply. You may contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your enquiry.

06 / 10

Security and retention

We take steps intended to safeguard personal information against unauthorised access, loss and misuse. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

We retain enquiry information and correspondence for legitimate business and record-keeping purposes and for periods required by applicable law. Requests concerning the information we hold can be made using the contact details below.

07 / 10

Access and correction

You may contact us to request access to personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or enquire about deletion. We will consider each request in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify your identity.

08 / 10

Cookies and similar technologies

Information about cookies and other browser technologies is available in our Cookie Policy.

09 / 10

Sensitive information and children

Please do not submit passwords, access credentials, sensitive personal information or confidential third-party material through the general enquiry form. The website is intended for professional and business enquiries and is not directed at children.

10 / 10

Contact, complaints and updates

For privacy enquiries, access or correction requests, or complaints about our handling of personal information, email alomaritech@gmail.com. We will review your enquiry and respond as reasonably practicable.

If Australian privacy law applies to a complaint, you may also have rights to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner after raising the matter with us. We may amend this policy to reflect changes to the website, our practices or applicable law; the revised date will appear above.
